YOUR CAREER TOOLKIT

Build a CV that opens doors

You don't need years of work experience to create a good CV. Learn how to present your education, skills, projects and experience in a way that helps employers understand what you can offer.

START HERE

Your CV is your introduction to an employer.

A good CV is clear, truthful and relevant to the opportunity you're applying for. You don't have to include everything about yourself. Focus on what makes you suitable for the role.

📄
STEP 1

What should your CV include?

Use this structure as a starting point when creating your CV.

01

Contact information

Make it easy for employers to contact you.

  • Full name
  • Professional email address
  • Contact number
  • Town or area where you are based
02

Personal profile

Introduce yourself in a short paragraph.

  • Your professional interests
  • Your strongest qualities
  • What you can contribute
  • Your career goals
03

Education and training

Show employers what you have studied.

  • Schools and institutions
  • Qualifications achieved
  • Courses and certificates
  • Current studies, if applicable
04

Work and practical experience

Demonstrate where you've gained experience.

  • Previous employment
  • Part-time or casual work
  • Volunteer activities
  • Relevant projects or internships
05

Skills and abilities

Highlight skills that relate to the job.

  • Computer and digital skills
  • Communication
  • Customer service
  • Technical or practical skills
06

References

Identify people who can speak about your work or character.

  • Previous supervisors
  • Teachers or lecturers
  • Mentors or project leaders
  • Ask permission before listing them
NO WORK EXPERIENCE?

You can still build a strong first CV.

Employers may also want to see your potential, initiative and willingness to learn. Consider including:

🎓 School and college projects

Research, assignments, presentations and practical work.

🤝 Community involvement

Volunteering, organising activities and helping community initiatives.

💡 Personal projects

Websites, designs, repairs, creative work or small business activities.

🏆 Leadership and achievements

School responsibilities, sports leadership and awards.

EXAMPLE PERSONAL PROFILE

"Motivated and reliable school leaver with good communication skills and a willingness to learn. I enjoy working with people, solving problems and contributing to a team. I am seeking an entry-level opportunity where I can develop my skills and gain practical workplace experience."

Example only. Applicants should adapt this to reflect their real qualities and goals.
STEP 2

Before you submit your CV

Use this checklist to review your application.

Remember: always follow the employer's instructions. Some vacancies require additional forms or supporting documents.

STEP 3

Apply smarter, not just more often.

Small improvements can make your applications more professional.

🔍

Read the job advert carefully

Understand the responsibilities, minimum requirements and application deadline.

🎯

Match your skills to the role

Highlight relevant experience and skills that genuinely match the vacancy.

✉️

Use a professional email

Include the job title or reference number in your subject line when requested.

📎

Submit the right documents

Check whether the employer requires a CV, cover letter, certificates or application form.
