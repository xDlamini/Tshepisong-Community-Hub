Our Story

Tshepisong —
A Name That Means Hope.

A township west of Johannesburg, built on community, resilience, and the spirit of togetherness.

Tshepisong is located in Roodepoort, west of Johannesburg. The name — derived from Sesotho — carries the meaning of hope, and hope is exactly what this community embodies every single day.

From its founding as a residential township, Tshepisong has grown into a vibrant, self-sustaining community of families, entrepreneurs, learners, faith communities and leaders. Our streets are alive with the sound of children, music, and business.

This website is a digital home — a place where our community can be seen, celebrated, and connected. We believe Tshepisong deserves to be on the map, and this is our first step in making that happen.
